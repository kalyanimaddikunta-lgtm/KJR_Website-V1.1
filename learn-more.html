Dark Store Architecture

Our hyper-local dark store nodes are structured inside major urban corridors to shorten the last-mile radius. Utilizing localized demand mapping and intelligent bin placement layouts, staff can process inbound picking and consolidation commands in under 3 minutes.

  • 3-Minute Pick & Sort SLA compliance
  • FIFO inventory tracking models
  • Ambient/Chilled compartmental storage

Transit Load Dispatching

Vehicles are dispatched based on density sequencing models. Driver terminals receive optimized drop-off sequences linked to real-time city routing patterns, eliminating detention wait times at central depots and maintaining delivery timelines.

  • Real-Time dynamic route redirection
  • Geofenced load lock and arrival alerts
  • Multi-drop delivery scheduling
